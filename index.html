About me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at Wayne State University. Prior to this appointment, I held a Postdoctoral Fellow position in the Department of Mathematic and Statistics at Auburn University under the guidance of Prof. Yanzhao Cao and a short-term Postdoctoral Associate position in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Cornell University, advised by Prof. Vikram Krishnamurthy. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the University of Connecticut under the supervision of Prof. George Yin. I obtained my B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2018.
