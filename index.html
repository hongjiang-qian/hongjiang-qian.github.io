About me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at Wayne State University. Prior to this appointment, I held a Postdoctoral Fellow position in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at Auburn University under the guidance of Prof. Yanzhao Cao, and a short-term Postdoctoral Associate position in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Cornell University, advised by Prof. Vikram Krishnamurthy. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the University of Connecticut under the supervision of Prof. George Yin, and my B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2018.

Research Interests

  • Stochastic Control and Optimization: Stochastic optimal control, stochastic approximation

  • Applied Probability and Stochastic Analysis: Stochastic (partial) differential equations, large and moderate deviations

  • Numerial Analysis and Data Science: Numerics for SDEs and SPDEs, mathematical statistics and data science
